PLFM Seminar

Usually Tuesdays at 14:00 — INR 331

An informal PL seminar open to all. The format is fluid: at each session, someone presents a research paper or idea they found interesting, demos their own work, dry runs a talk they are preparing, or proposes a talk to watch together — and there is always plenty of free-form discussion during and after the presentation. There is no “requirement” or set schedule to present, as we usually have enough spontaneous proposals. Take a look at the sort of topics we’ve had in the recent past in the Schedule tab.

To receive updates, subscribe to the mailing list by sending an email to pl-seminar-subscribe@listes.epfl.ch.
