Title: Dependent Types for Distributed Systems
Date: 2026-03-15 14:00
Category: seminar
Speaker: John Roe
Affiliation: University of Example
Location: INJ 114
Upcoming: true

We present a dependently-typed programming language designed for reasoning about distributed systems. Our type system captures key properties such as message ordering, fault tolerance, and eventual consistency, enabling compile-time verification of distributed protocols.
